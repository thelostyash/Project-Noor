# EPIC 01 — Product Discovery & Requirements (Sprint S0)
**Project:** Noor — Reflective Wellness & Non-Clinical Companion  
**Status:** Validated  
**Platform Target:** Responsive Web / Progressive Web App (PWA)

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Value Proposition

### The Problem
Modern digital wellness tools suffer from two extremes:
1. **Clinical / Cold Medicalization:** Apps that immediately pathologize emotional fluctuation with psychiatric symptom checklists (PHQ-9 / GAD-7), creating cognitive strain, shame, or fear of diagnosis.
2. **Addictive / High-Alert Productivity Gamification:** Apps that demand daily streaks, aggressive push notifications, public leaderboards, or manipulative companion illness states ("Your pet will be sad if you don't journal today").

### The Solution: Noor
Noor is an open, non-clinical sanctuary bridging self-expression, gentle play, authentic human connection, and companion evolution.
- **Calm by Default:** No pressure, no word counts, no unsolicited AI diagnostic interventions.
- **Need-State Driven:** Adapts dynamically to how the user feels ("I want to write", "I want to relax", "I want to connect").
- **Strict Anti-Dependency:** The companion never suffers, wilts, or complains when users step away.

## 3. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

1. **Expressive Grounding:** *"When I feel mentally cluttered, I want a quiet, distraction-free space to pour out my thoughts so that I can establish a safe boundary between myself and anxiety."*
2. **Tactile Decompression:** *"When I lack the energy to write or talk, I want gentle visual and tactile interactions (Breathing flower, mood painting, tile sliding) so that my nervous system can down-regulate without judgment."*
3. **Safe Connectedness:** *"When I feel isolated, I want to read real, uncurated reflections from other human beings so that I feel less alone, without being sucked into doomscrolling."*

---

## 4. Measurable MVP Success Criteria

- **Activation:** 80%+ of new users complete a gentle action (note, activity, or check-in) in their first session.
- **Safety Integrity:** 100% of crisis or high-risk inputs trigger the non-stigmatizing crisis support path.
- **Perceived Calm:** 85%+ user rating of feeling respected and unpressured.
- **Consent Comprehension:** 90%+ of surveyed users can accurately state whether their notes are used for model training (strict NO).
